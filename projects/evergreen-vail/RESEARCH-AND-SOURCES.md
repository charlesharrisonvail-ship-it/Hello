# The Evergreen, Vail: research ledger

Compiled 2026-11-02 for a possible EpiVail pitch. Status: **on hold**. The
developer has its own brokerage, and the project is in litigation (see below).

## The project

- Redevelopment of the 1970s Evergreen Lodge hotel and the 19 Vail Inn condos
  in the same building, next to the main Vail Health hospital. The new building
  is "The Evergreen."
- About 110 high-end condos (109 in the 2023 approvals) and 48,000 sq ft of
  amenity space: rooftop and basement pools, two pickleball courts, golf
  simulators, a gym, a full spa, and a lobby bar and restaurant. Owners may
  rent their units to the public. The developer calls it an "Aman-esque"
  resort. (Vail Daily, 2026-06-03)
- Approved by Vail's Planning and Environmental Commission (Nov 2022) and
  Design Review Board. The council unanimously upheld the PEC approval on
  appeal by Vail International. No further council approval is needed.

## Developer

- Peter Knobel's Solaris Group, which owns the site through HCT Development
  (acquired 2012). Attorney for Solaris Group: Matt Morr.
- **Solaris Real Estate**, a Vail brokerage tied to Knobel (Knobel listed as an
  owner; agent Brian Butts lists units at the Residences at Solaris). This
  suggests sales would likely be handled in-house. Not confirmed for The
  Evergreen.

## Status and timeline

- 2026-05/06: Condo owners voted 88.3% to terminate and sell. Judge Paul
  Dunkelman declined to nullify the vote or grant an injunction. Two holdout
  owners (Peter Sallerson; George and Mary Cavanaugh) continue to litigate,
  and their attorney said they're "in litigation for the foreseeable future."
- 2026-07-15 hearing: Solaris's attorney said delays moved the start "from
  starting last April to now starting this coming April," at a "$20 million
  increase with our general contractor." That likely means April 2027 (an
  inference).
- No pricing, presales or broker arrangement has been published.
