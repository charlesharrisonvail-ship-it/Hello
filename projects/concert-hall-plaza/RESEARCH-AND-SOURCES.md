# Concert Hall Plaza / The Apogean, Vail: research ledger

Compiled 2026-11-02 for a possible EpiVail pitch. Status: **no listing pitch.**
The residences are already being sold through listing brokers (see below). The
open angle is buyer-side only.

## The project

- **Address:** 616 W. Lionshead Circle, Lionshead, Vail. It replaces the
  1970s Concert Hall Plaza building.
- **Name:** The Apogean (apogeanvail.com), "boutique resort living." There are
  nine private residences in a six-story building on an 11,291 sq ft site. Plans
  run three to five bedrooms. Amenities: an underground automated valet garage,
  a restaurant space, a fitness studio, and a thermal spa (steam, sauna, cold
  plunge, hot tub). The sales showroom is at Landmark Vail, 610 W. Lionshead
  Circle.
- **Developer entity in the town agreement:** Beaver Capital SPC LLC (also
  reported as "SPE"). The developer's people are not confirmed. A town update
  may have named Triumph Development West, but that could be a mix-up with
  Timber Ridge; it is unverified (the vail.gov pages refused access).
- **Planner:** Dominic Mauriello, Mauriello Planning Group.

## Approvals

- Planning and Environmental Commission: unanimous approval in September 2025,
  with six conditions, including employee-housing mitigation for 245 net sq ft.
- Design Review Board: approved April 2026.
- Town Council: development agreement approved 4-3 on 2026-07-21 (Resolution 26,
  Series 2026), clearing the way for a building permit. The walkway narrows from
  15 to 13 feet. 10 businesses are reduced to room for 2 (including the Little
  Diner).
