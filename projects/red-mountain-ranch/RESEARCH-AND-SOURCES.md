# Red Mountain Ranch: research ledger

Compiled 2026-10-31 for an EpiVail marketing pitch. These are dated findings from
public filings and news, not confirmation from the developer. Reverify before
anything goes public.

## The project

- **Name:** Red Mountain Ranch (RMR), Parcel 1 (Planning Area 1 & OS-2).
- **Address:** 17500 US-6, Eagle, CO 81631. On the north bank of the Eagle
  River, east-northeast of U.S. 6 and Nogal Road.
- **Size:** 17.5 acres (parcel 1939-331-18-002). Adjoining parcels on the plan:
  OS-3, 15.4 acres; OS-1, 1.8 acres (park to be dedicated to the town).
- **Homes:** 66 in 26 buildings: 42 townhomes (8 buildings, 4 types), 12 duplex
  homes (6 buildings), 12 single-family (12 buildings, 2 types). About 218,420
  sq ft of new homes. The recorded PUD (Sep 15, 2020) allows 97; density was cut
  by 31 to honor the 2015 Eagle River Corridor Plan.
- **Design:** two-car garages for every home. Main two-way road off U.S. 6 for
  the townhomes; a one-way inner road for the duplexes and single-family homes.
  Open space preserved at the core. A 75-foot river setback (Vail Daily: 25 feet
  beyond the 50-foot requirement). The public Discovery Trail runs through the
  setback and links all six parcels. Native planting that needs no irrigation
  once established. No fences or yards (Vail Daily).
- **Energy:** all-electric, air-source heat pumps, ERV, envelope 10% better than
  code, 30% of roof space for solar, renewable power via Holy Cross Energy.
- **Bedrooms:** every home three or four bedrooms (developer, Vail Daily).
- **Price, tenure, deed restrictions:** not published. Developer: "too early to
  say." He also said some buyers might use homes as second homes ("Maybe").

## Approval status

- PUD recorded 2020-09-15. Sketch subdivision 2024-01-24. MDP and preliminary
  plan submitted 2025-01-13; revised preliminary plan narrative 2025-09-17/22.
- **Eagle Town Council "key approval" on 2026-03-24** (Vail Daily, 2026-04-02).
  The specific approvals, vote and conditions were not reported. It still needs
  "additional permitting and final plat approvals before breaking ground." No
  construction dates were announced.

## People (from public filings and reporting)

- **Owner/developer:** Griffin Development, LLC. Primary contact Rodrigo
  Cortina, rcortina@pegaso.net, 970.331.3736, 701 W. Lionshead Cir., Vail, CO
  81657 (cover sheet G-00, Jan 2025). Planning documents also name Eagle River
  Commercial LLC and the Red Mountain Ranch Partnership LLLP as owners.
- Cortina (Vail Daily): Parcel 1 is "the first of six parcels on the river that
  are part of this PUD — I'm involved with all of them — and so the idea is
  proof of concept here."
- **Architect / general contractor:** Tres Birds (Denver): Mike Moore, Doug
  Newby, David Hoffman. **Landscape:** Wenk Associates.
- **Town:** Kyle Brotherton, Interim Community Development Director (as of
  April 2026).
