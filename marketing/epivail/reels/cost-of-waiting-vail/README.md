## Script (Eva)

> Rent in the Vail Valley? Well, bless its heart. A three-bedroom in Eagle
> averages thirty-six hundred dollars a month. That's forty-three thousand, two
> hundred dollars a year... gone. Nothing back but receipts. Now say you buy a
> typical Eagle townhome, about seven hundred thousand, with five percent down
> at today's seven point two eight percent. Your first year, you pay down about
> sixty-four hundred dollars of your own loan. Money that stays yours, plus
> whatever the home gains. Now, owning costs more each month up here, and I
> won't pretend it doesn't. But every rent check builds somebody else's yard.
> And Olaf would sure like his own. Comment below, and follow for more.
