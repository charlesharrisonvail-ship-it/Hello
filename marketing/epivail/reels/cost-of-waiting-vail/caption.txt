Y'all, rent in the Vail Valley is no joke. 🏔️

A 3-bedroom in Eagle averages $3,600 a month. That's $43,200 a year, and not a penny of it comes back to you.

Buy the median Eagle townhome instead ($702,200, 5% down, 7.28% rate), and in year one about $6,400 of your loan gets paid down. That money stays yours.

I won't sugarcoat it: owning costs more each month up here. But every rent check builds somebody else's yard. Olaf would like his own. 🐾
