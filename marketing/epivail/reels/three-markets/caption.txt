3 Christmas markets. 3 mugs. 1 winner. 🎄☕

🏰 Heidelberg after dark: carousels, twinkling lights, Glühwein date night, Santa on the ride, and gondola huts that felt like home.

🏙️ Mannheim by the Wasserturm: the big Christmas pyramid, a sunny morning stroll through the stalls, and Santa (again).

🛡️ Rothenburg ob der Tauber: storybook streets, Christmas shops packed with Santas, snow globes, nutcrackers and cuckoo clocks, beer steins fit for a king, an antique shop full of history, the medieval stocks, a knight who crashed our selfie, and a sunset over the rooftops. 🌅

Every market sends you home with its mug. So you tell me: which one wins? Comment 1, 2 or 3, and follow for more. 👇
