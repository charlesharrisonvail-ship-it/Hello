# 3 Christmas Markets, 3 Mugs (EpiVail lifestyle reel)

Vertical 1080×1920, 62.4 s, 2026-11-25. Heidelberg (night, 11/23), Mannheim (day, 11/24),
Rothenburg ob der Tauber (11/25). Each market closes on its souvenir mug with a "MUG No. X"
stamp; the finale lines the three up and asks "Which mug wins? Comment 1, 2 or 3".

- Music (Charles asked for traditional German Christmas music): J. S. Bach, Christmas
  Oratorio BWV 248, "Jauchzet, frohlocket!", performed by Phiroz Dalal with St Matthew's
  Concert Choir and Orchestra, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0
  (commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bach_-_Christmas_Oratorio_(Dalal)_-_01_Jauchzet,_frohlokket.ogg).
  The license requires credit: the caption carries it. Starts on the opening timpani;
  cuts follow its beat (`beats.json`, ~128 BPM).

- Mozart's Sleigh Ride (K. 605 No. 3) was the first choice, but no free-to-use recording
  was reachable (Musopen needs a login); a commercial recording risks a muted post.
- Rothenburg section (added from Charles's second upload): Plönlein, Herrngasse-area
  street, medieval stocks at the Kriminalmuseum ("behave… or else" — not "still in use"),
  giant nutcracker photo, Christmas shop, knight selfie, tower steps, then mug No. 3.
