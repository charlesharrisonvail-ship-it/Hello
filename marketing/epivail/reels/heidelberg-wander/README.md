# Sunday Office: Heidelberg (EpiVail lifestyle reel)

Vertical 1080×1920, 29 s, made 2026-11-22 from Charles's 16 clips and 4 photos shot in
Heidelberg that day. Lifestyle piece: travel as part of the life, not an EpiVail pitch
(the brand shows only as the corner mark and the end card).

- Beat-cut montage to "House Vibez" by Lily J (Mixkit Stock Music Free License,
  assets.mixkit.co/music/745), 123 BPM; every cut on a beat (`beats.json`), the music lift
  lands on the one-beat Christmas-window run. SFX: Mixkit, via video-shotcraft.
- Footage graded warm (it came in flat/gray), cropped to 9:16, slow pushes, whip blurs on
  4 cuts, photos shown as dropped prints with a shutter sound.
- No voiceover, so no Higgsfield credits spent. Master: -13.9 LUFS, -2.1 dBFS peak.
- The bridge-monkey plaque line is a loose translation of "Was thustu mich hie angaffen?"
- `thumbnail-9x16.jpg` / `thumbnail-square.jpg` for Lofty; `caption.txt` for the post.
- One Christmas-run beat shows a shop's own sign (Inge-Glas) in its window.
- End card (2026-11-22 revision): "HEIDELBERG · PART ONE / MORE TO COME! / stay tuned, y'all…" at Charles's request, to leave viewers wanting more; part two to follow.
