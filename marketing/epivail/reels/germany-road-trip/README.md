# Germany, Part Two: Road Trip (EpiVail lifestyle reel)

Vertical 1080×1920, 20.2 s, made 2026-11-24 from Charles's clips and photos that day:
Mannheim (Wasserturm Christmas market, market mug), a Turley Barracks stop (no footage;
shown as a label on the animated route map), Worms (Nibelungen bridge tower, cathedral),
back to Heidelberg (church, Vetter's Brauhaus, hotel at dusk). Follows part one
(`../heidelberg-wander/`) in the same look and ends on "MORE TO COME!".
