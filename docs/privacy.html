Privacy Policy

AiRE Estate, LLC · Effective September 25, 2026

What we collect

When you buy a course, our payment processor collects your name, email address, and payment details to complete the sale. We receive your name, email, and purchase record. We never receive or store your full card number.

How we use it

We use your information only to deliver your course, provide support, send receipts, and keep business and tax records. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

On this website

This site does not use advertising trackers. Your course progress, quiz results and access code are saved only in your own browser on your own device (browser storage). They are never sent to us. Our web host and video host keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to operate and secure the service.

Service providers

We use these providers, and each handles your information only to provide its service: Stripe processes payments (Stripe privacy policy); GitHub Pages hosts this website and keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses (GitHub privacy statement); a video hosting service delivers course video; and Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, which means your browser contacts Google to load them (Google privacy policy).

Email

We email you only about your purchase and your questions. We don’t send marketing email unless you ask to hear from us, and you can stop any email by replying or writing to hello@aireestate.com.

How long we keep it

We keep purchase records as long as tax and accounting rules require, and delete other personal information you ask us to delete.

Children

This site and our courses are for real estate professionals and are not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, except records we must keep by law. Email hello@aireestate.com.
