What does it actually cost to make an AI listing video?

AiRE Estate Academy guide · Checked November 3, 2026

Three separate costs, and people mix them up: what you pay to learn it, the subscriptions you pay monthly, and the credits each video uses. Here’s each one, with the math.

1. Tuition: once

Whatever course you take, if any. Ours, Listing Media Without the Invoice, is $67 once. You can also learn it free by trial and error. A course saves you the errors.

2. Subscriptions: monthly

ToolWhat it doesPrice
Claude Pro (includes Claude Code)Runs the workflow: picks the shots, writes the prompts, stitches the video$20 a month from Anthropic
HiggsfieldGenerates the video clipsStarter is $19 a month billed yearly, for 270 credits a month
ffmpegJoins the clips into one videoFree
Apify (optional)Pulls photos from a listing linkFree tier available

3. Usage credits: per video

This is the part that changes with every listing. On Higgsfield’s pricing page, a five-second Kling 3.0 clip at 1080p uses about 8 credits.

VideoClipsCreditsAbout
One aerial-style clip from one photo1about 8$0.56
Room-by-room showcase, 10 rooms10about 80$5.60
Same showcase, with one redo on three rooms13about 104$7.30

Dollar figures use the Starter plan’s yearly price: $19 × 12 ÷ (270 × 12) is about $0.07 a credit. Monthly billing and other models cost more per credit; premium models such as Veo 3.1 use 40 credits per 4 seconds. Redos are normal, so budget for a few.

The honest total

For an agent making one showcase a month: $20 (Claude Pro) + $19 (Higgsfield Starter, billed yearly), and the credits for one showcase fit inside that plan. That’s about $39 a month in subscriptions after a one-time $67 for the course. Premium listing packages are publicly reported at $400 to $950 per listing (what those include), but they include platform placement and a 3D tour, which this doesn’t.

Before you publish any AI video
Watch every frame and cut anything that isn’t true to the property. Label it as AI-generated. Use only photos you have the rights to.

Sources, checked November 3, 2026: Higgsfield pricing (affiliate link); Claude Pro price from Anthropic. Prices are set by those companies and change.
