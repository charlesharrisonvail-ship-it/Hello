Software is a separate bill, wherever you study

None of these prices include the AI tools themselves. Claude Pro is $20 a month from Anthropic. Video tools charge by the credit. Ask any course, ours included, which subscriptions you’ll need and what a typical project costs in credits. We break that down in What does it actually cost to make an AI listing video?

Which one fits you

  • You want designation credit. RRC is the only one here that counts toward the CRS. We don’t offer CE or designation credit.
  • You want a live person answering questions as you go. VDCI includes instructor messaging. We answer by email but don’t offer one-on-one coaching.
  • You want the theory: machine learning, data and AI strategy. Columbia goes much deeper. It was closed to enrollment when we checked.
  • You have one job to get done this week, like a listing to launch or a seller to update, and want to see the result before you pay. That’s what we built. Each course does one job, shows you the finished result on its page, and costs $67 or $97.
