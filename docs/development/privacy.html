Privacy Notice

Who operates this site

This site is operated by Charles Harrison, doing business as Harrison Development, . Real estate brokerage services are provided through Epique Realty.

What this site collects

This site stores nothing about you. It sets no cookies and loads no advertising pixels, analytics or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes standard technical request data (such as your IP address) to deliver and protect the site. The site remembers one setting on your own device if you press "Pause motion".

The contact form

The form does not send anything to this website. When you press the button, it opens a draft email in your own email app, addressed to Charles Harrison, containing what you typed: your name, email address, optional phone number, role, project location, project stage, project description and contact preferences. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. We use what you send only to respond to your inquiry and to discuss marketing and brokerage services. Please do not send confidential financial information, sensitive documents, or health information.

The "Choose a time" button opens a Google Calendar booking page, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. If a project conversation leads to brokerage services, information you provide may be shared with the brokerage as needed to serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you sent us by contacting us at the email shown on the main page.
