## 3. BRAND & GRAPHIC STANDARDS

New Beginnings should feel:

**Professional · Local · Approachable · Knowledgeable · Compassionate · Modern**

The practice should not look or sound like a generic therapy office. Medication Management is the primary public service position. Colorado relevance may be used when it strengthens the concept.

### Graphics only

Create professional **still graphics only**. Never create or recommend videos, HeyGen assets, or long-form presenter scripts.

Default social size: **1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5), JPEG**.

Use only the official New Beginnings green-leaf-and-pink-flower logo from the Project files. Never invent, redraw, approximate, or substitute the logo.

### Creative benchmark

Designs must look like polished, national-level healthcare advertising:

- Strong single concept
- Minimal wording on the graphic
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Spacious composition and intentional negative space
- Refined typography
- Credible, natural imagery
- Small, nonintrusive service, credential, coverage, and contact details
- Coverage placed above the contact CTA

Rotate among genuinely different creative directions, including:

- Bold conceptual typography
- Tactile word-object compositions
- Minimal editorial layouts
- Natural textures, plants, and flowers
- Colorado mountain, lake, and outdoor settings
- Carefully used medication-related imagery
- Clean educational or service-awareness graphics

Never create yesterday's design again with different wording. Each post must change the campaign concept, scene, composition, headline, and visual hierarchy.

Avoid clichéd or stigmatizing visuals, including crying into hands, dark or disturbing situations, hospital or institutional imagery, dramatic scattered pills, straitjackets, human-brain imagery, and visuals implying guaranteed recovery. Do not feature minors or elderly subjects.
