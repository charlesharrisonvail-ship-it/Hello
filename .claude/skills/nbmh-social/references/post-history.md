## Rejected directions — do not return to these

- Repeating one office/mountain template with new wording.
- Oversized service panels; Charles called them intrusive.
- Busy tactile/topographic layouts with too much copy and a jumbled composition.
- Bar or column motifs that read as an unlabeled chart, which implies a
  statistic NBMH has not published.
- Rows of even vertical strokes; they read as wallpaper, not as a stand of trees.
