Charles asked on 2026-10-21 for AiRE Estate Instagram and TikTok pages, and for
an Instagram reel's "20 Claude skills" pack (Mohammed Farshan, @farshanedits).
That pack is only sent by DM to people who comment on the reel; it is not public,
and nobody here has read it. Two AiRE-only skills were built instead:

- `aire-social` - captions, hooks, reel scripts, carousels, Stories, plans,
  repurposing and audits, with every AiRE rule built in.
- `aire-reel-edit` - `reel.py`, free and ffmpeg-only: ten edit styles and three
  cover looks standing in for his twelve (mapped by name and thumbnail), captions
  timed from a script against the audio's pauses (no transcription), "Made with
  AI" tag, covers. Fonts fall back to DejaVu Sans in the cloud box.

Charles creates the accounts himself (phone code and birthday);
`marketing/social/instagram-tiktok-setup.md` has the steps, bios and handle
order. Instagram connects to the AiRE Estate Facebook page in the Aire Estate
business portfolio. If he later sends the Farshan PDF or skills, read them all
before installing anything.
