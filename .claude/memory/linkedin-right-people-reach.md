**EpiVail LinkedIn only.** Charles asked for this to drive more traffic
(2026-11-03). Source: LinkedIn Guide to Creating, "Get Your Content in Front of
the Right People" (Create Possibilities newsletter, 2026-11-03). It cites no
statistics; the steps are its advice, not measured results.

Thesis: fifty views from people who could hire (or join) you beat ten thousand
from people who can't.

1. Name the reader before writing.
2. Find what they're stuck on (their comments, questions, arguments).
3. Write about that problem; give the answer away; help more than promote.
4. Comment on posts the audience already reads - "the fastest reach on the
   platform," about two minutes each. Draft only; Charles posts.
5. Watch who responds - score reach by right-audience engagers, not impressions.
6. Say the thing only you would say - broad topics attract, the specific take
   makes them follow.
