description: the minimum production standard for any video, Reel or Short made for Charles (EpiVail or AiRE Estate); check every video against it before calling it done
---

Charles set this bar on 2026-10-31 with a reference Reel (screenshot in
`assets/reference/video-minimum-bar.jpg`, a third-party creator's post about
Claude Code making phone calls; it is a style reference only, never reused).
"This is the minimum you should do on videos."

Every video has, at minimum:

1. **A kinetic headline with type contrast.** It is large and bold, builds on
   screen word by word, and at least one key word switches to an italic serif in
   the brand accent color (for example "Claude writes the *call*,").
2. **Floating proof chips.** Small pill labels near the headline carry hard
   facts or specs ("sub-400 ms replies", "40+ languages"). They are slightly
   rotated, use mixed colors, and pop in one at a time. Every number on a chip
   is sourced.
3. **A diagram that explains the mechanism.** Icon tiles joined by an arrow
   labeled with the real action (`create_call`), plus a tag naming the system
   ("AI phone platform"). Show how it works, not just that it works.
4. **A code or UI card.** A dark panel showing the real call, prompt or screen,
   typed on in a monospace font. Never an invented UI; for AiRE Estate, show
   the proof without giving away the method (verbatim prompts stay private).
5. **A presenter on screen.** A character or person who points at the
   content and guides the eye. Never a synthesized likeness of Charles unless he
   asks for one, and AiRE Estate public videos never front his name.
6. **Texture and depth.** A halftone or grain background, drop shadows and
   layered elements, never a flat empty slide.
7. **Burned-in captions and a vertical 9:16 frame**, with the layout kept clear
   of the platform's side buttons and bottom caption area.
8. **Motion on every beat.** Something moves or appears at least every 1 to 2
   seconds; nothing static for long.

The brand still decides colors, fonts, voice and sign-off: EpiVail rules for
EpiVail videos, AiRE Estate rules for AiRE Estate. This is a floor, not a
ceiling.
