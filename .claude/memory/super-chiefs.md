Built 2026-11-23 at Charles's request: "I need recruits, leads, and traffic... keep
them separate, each pumping and learning and evolving as super chiefs of staff."
Blueprint: `plans/super-chiefs-blueprint.md`.

- **EpiVail** -> `epivail-chief` (runs lead-enrichment, lofty-crm, recruitment-outreach,
  linkedin-content). North star: recruit conversations booked per week.
- **AiRE Estate** -> `aire-chief` + `aire-content`. North star: site visits from posts,
  paid enrollments per week.
- **Harrison Development** -> `hd-chief` + `hd-prospector`. North star: developer/landowner

Each chief is the only agent Charles talks to for that business, refuses the other two
businesses' work, checks drafts against its own brand list, and stops for his "go" before
any send, post, deploy, Lofty write or credit spend.

Learning loop: Friday weekly review per team writes counts and one lesson to
`.claude/teams/<team>/playbook.md`; a lesson that holds three weeks moves into the agent
file. Playbooks are public: counts and patterns only.
