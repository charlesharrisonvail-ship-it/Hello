On 2026-11-18 Charles asked to install Collage B-Roll, HyperFrames, One take,
Claude Animation Base and Lemo-Opuscar from GitHub and make them all part of his
normal video making skill. That skill is `.claude/skills/video-making/`: the front
door for any video request; it routes to the right tool, including his own
`it-reelsmaker` plugin and `remotion-epivail-video` account skill.

- Free, code-rendered: Take One, HyperFrames (local skills), Lemo-Opuscar
  (plugin `lemo-opuscar@lemolab`), Claude Animation Base (`tools/claude-animation-base/`).
- Paid: Collage B-Roll generates through MaxFusion. Only its metaphor gate is
  free; the free collage look is HyperFrames `style-sampling` (Collage Explainer).
- Clawd, the character in Claude Animation Base, is Anthropic's - keep it out of
  his brand videos.
