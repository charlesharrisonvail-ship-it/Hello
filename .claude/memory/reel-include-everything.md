Charles, 2026-11-26: "U left stuff out...stop doing that. This is not a race...it's my life."

When he sends footage or photos for a reel, include every clip and every photo he sent
for it, across all his uploads (the chat cuts big uploads, so he often sends in several
messages). Do not drop shots to hit a length, and do not drop ones that seem off-theme
(antique helmets, a statue, souvenir shelves): they were his day.

If something truly can't be used (unreadable, or a rights problem such as someone
else's poster as the subject), say so plainly and ask; never quietly leave it out.

Length follows the footage. With the pacing rule in `reel-energy.md` (each shot ~2.3 s,
photos ~3 s), a full day can run two minutes, which is fine.

Charles, 2026-11-26 (again): "u left out all the good Christmas Market footage." Every clip
was in, but trimmed to ~2.7 s slices. Including a clip means showing its good part in full:
highlight footage (markets, lights, people, food, the moment he filmed it for) plays at its
own length. Only filler/transition clips get the short hold. When unsure, show more.
