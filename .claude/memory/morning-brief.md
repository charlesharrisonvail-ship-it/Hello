Charles does not want a dashboard. On 2026-10-24 a dark "Tron" dashboard
(Lofty, Gmail, AiRE Estate, social zones, terminal links) was built; he said it
"felt weird" and deleted it. He chose a **daily morning brief email** instead.

- Sent to charlesharrisonvail@gmail.com from his own Gmail, about 6:47am Mountain,
  every day, by a scheduled task ("Morning Brief"). It only reads Lofty, Gmail and
  Google Calendar and sends that one email to him - never to anyone else.
- Short, scannable, courteous: "Needs you today" first, then Lofty, Email, AiRE
  Estate, Calendar. Plain text with a few headers, no tables, no hype.
- EpiVail and AiRE Estate stay in separate sections. Do-not-contact names in
  `CLAUDE.md` are left out entirely.
- He also uses Lofty's own home screen for lead work.
