Positioning (Charles, 2026-11-02): he is an Area/Growth Leader with Epique Realty
who paves the way with AI workflows and instructs AI classes in the real estate
industry. Lead bios and profile copy with that: leader, AI innovator, educator.
Do not reduce him to a local Vail Valley agent.

Tone: "Southern manners" means courteous, gracious, polished. It never means folksy
or colloquial. No "Howdy", "folks", "visit with you", "it would be my pleasure".
He called that draft "a hick". Write like a confident executive who is gracious.

On EpiVail materials, don't name AiRE Estate (the two businesses stay separate);
"instructs AI classes for real estate professionals" is fine.
