Charles found a calm, slide-by-slide reel "so boring I fell asleep" (2026-11-20).
Every reel must use the design/motion skills he installed (viral-hooks,
video-shotcraft, choreograph, it-reelsmaker) from the first draft, not after a complaint.

What he approved in its place (marketing/epivail/reels/cost-of-waiting-vail/):
- Hook lands visually in the first second (big number rolling/slamming).
- PACING (Charles, 2026-11-26, "most of your reels are too fast and fans cannot see the
  pics or vids"): every photo or clip holds at least ~2.3 s (photos/mugs ~3 s). Never cut
  one shot per beat, no rapid-fire runs, no whip pans between ordinary shots; use soft
  crossfades and slow 2-9% pushes. Energy comes from music, captions and sound, not cut
  speed. Travel/footage reels may run ~60 s to give each shot its time.
- On text/graphic explainers (like the rent reel) counters and animated cards are fine,
  but still give each number time to read.
- Sound design on every visual event (impacts, whooshes, ticks, a stamp hit) over an
  upbeat track; shotcraft's Mixkit bgm/sfx are free to use. Max 3 screen shakes.
- Eva reads short, punchy lines with energy ("Y'all...", "Poof. Gone.", "Yep.").
- Bold karaoke captions; still honest numbers with sources on the end card.
