name: agent-roster
description: Which subagent to reach for, and the order they chain in
---

- `lead-enrichment` - a name, company, LinkedIn URL, email, or vague
  description becomes a full contact card. Depth over breadth: one
  fully-enriched lead beats five half-enriched ones.
- `recruitment-outreach` - cold emails, LinkedIn DMs, SMS, call scripts,
  multi-touch sequences for recruiting agents.
- `linkedin-content` - posts, content calendars, carousel and Reel concepts.
- `lofty-crm` - contacts, tiers, tags, recruit pipeline stages, follow-up tasks.

**They chain in this order:** enrich first, then load into Lofty, then write the
message the Lofty task calls for. Writing outreach to an un-enriched lead is
backwards - personalization requires knowing something true about the recipient.

`linkedin-optimizer` (skill) measures; `linkedin-content` (agent) creates.
Creation follows measurement: if analytics are in the conversation, let the data
pick topic and format.
