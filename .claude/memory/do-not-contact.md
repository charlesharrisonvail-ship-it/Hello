The list itself lives in `CLAUDE.md` under **Do not contact**. It is
deliberately not copied here: a duplicate that drifts gives false assurance, and
the list in `CLAUDE.md` is always loaded.

The rule is absolute. Never email, text, call, or enroll anyone on it in any
sequence - even when Lofty flags them as past due or needing attention.

Before sending any message to a lead:

1. Check the `CLAUDE.md` list.
2. Confirm the lead is still active. A Lofty "past due" or "needs attention"
   flag alone is never a reason to send.
3. When in doubt, draft it and show Charles instead of sending.
