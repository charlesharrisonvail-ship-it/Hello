01 / The opportunity

A future built
around belonging.

Dowd Junction sits at the intersection of mountain life and everyday life. Public planning identifies the former CDOT housing site along the Eagle River as an opportunity for a new community-housing neighborhood.

EpiVail’s vision is to give that opportunity a clear identity, an engaging digital home, and a direct connection to the people who want to live here.

Are these homes available for purchase?

No sales offering is presented here. Whether the development will offer individual ownership, rentals or a combination has not been confirmed for this concept. There are no reservations or deposits being accepted through this website.

Is EpiVail the appointed listing representative?

This is an independent EpiVail marketing proposal. No appointment, exclusive listing agreement, or affiliation with the Town of Avon or Corum is represented.

Why do some documents show 96 homes and others 100?

The 2025 Three-Mile Plan describes approximately 96 deed-restricted homes. The July 2026 planning packet describes a goal of 100 initial-phase homes and 200 additional homes north of the river. These are planning figures, not final approved inventory.

What is Corum’s role at Dowd Junction?

A January 2026 Vail Home Partners report documents off-site storage at Dowd Junction for Corum’s Southface Vail project. It does not establish Corum as the appointed developer of the future housing described here. That role remains to be confirmed.
