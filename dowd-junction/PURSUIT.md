# Pursuit plan: winning the Dowd Junction business

Drafted October 29, 2026. **Nothing here has been sent.** Charles sends, after
checking the "Do not contact" list in the repo's `CLAUDE.md` (nobody below is on it).

## Who decides

The record points to the **Town of Avon**, not Corum:

- Avon signed the contract to buy the land (reported August 2026). It will annex it,
  zone it and subdivide the first parcel.
- Its draft housing plan lists the first parcel's developer as **"Public/Private"**.
  No partner has been named, so one will probably be chosen through a Town process
  (RFP, RFQ or negotiated partnership).
- Whoever runs lease-up or sales marketing will be chosen either by the Town directly
  or by the developer it selects. The way in is to be **known to the Town before that
  process starts**, and known to whoever bids.

Corum is worth one courteous question. Its sign there is explained by the Southface
Vail soil storage, so it may have no role at all.

## Fit the pitch to the likely tenure

The plan's table lists Parcel 1 as **104 price-capped rentals at 100% AMI**. If that
holds, the job is a **lease-up**: an interest list, eligibility pre-screening support,
a fair application or lottery process, and move-in scheduling. Open with that, and say
we would be just as ready for a for-sale launch.

**Fair housing:** deed-restricted housing marketing has to reach the whole eligible
community fairly. Offering an affirmative, multilingual outreach plan (confirm with the Town which
languages its households need) is a real differentiator, not just compliance.

## What to do, in order

1. **Take the hero photo yourself.** A ground shot of the cleared site from public right
   of way (the kayak put-in or the Highway 6 shoulder), early or late light. Do not go
   onto the site. It is the single biggest upgrade the concept can get.
2. **Watch the public calendar.** Look at Avon Town Council and PZC agendas, the State
   Land Board closing, the annexation hearing, and any developer RFP/RFQ. Ask the Town
   to add you to the notice list (draft A asks).
3. **Send draft A** to the Town's housing planner. It asks for information and offers to
   help; it does not ask for business. Copy the Community Development Director only if
   Charles knows him.
4. **Send draft B** to Corum's general inbox: one question about whether it has a role.
5. **Meet in person, then show the concept.** Do not send the link cold. In the room it
   shows the care; forwarded, it could read as presumption. Open it on a tablet.
6. **When a developer is chosen,** send them the concept within a week with draft C.

## Questions to bring to the first meeting

- Tenure for Parcel 1: rental, for sale, or a mix? Which AMI bands?
- Who administers eligibility and the lottery or waitlist: the Town, a housing
  authority, or the developer?
- Timeline for closing, annexation, the developer RFP and groundbreaking?
- What branding or naming is the Town willing to let a partner propose?
- How has lease-up or sales marketing gone on the Town's past projects (for example
  Avondale or Lot 8), and what would it do differently?
