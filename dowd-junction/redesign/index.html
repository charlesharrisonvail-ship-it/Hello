01 · The place

Where the valley
narrows to a river.

For sixty years this ground served the highway. The Colorado Department of Transportation has run a service yard and employee housing here on State Land Board property since the early 1960s.

The eastern housing area has since been vacated so that community housing can follow, while the western part is still a CDOT service yard. The Town of Avon's 2025 Three-Mile Plan describes water and sewer service that can be reached without major new extensions.

It is a rare setting for workforce housing: on the river, along the Highway 6 corridor between EagleVail and the Dowd Junction interchange, minutes from the towns its residents serve.

Sources: Avon Three-Mile Plan, May 2025, printed pp. 9–10. Travel times, river access and amenities are not yet established and are not claimed here.

Three hundred homes,
in two chapters.

300 homes, goal
  1. May 2025

    Avon's Three-Mile Plan names the State Land Board land as eligible for annexation, with about 96 deed-restricted homes called for on the vacated CDOT housing area.

  2. Aug 2025

    The State Land Board withdraws its 2023 petition and files a new one for a smaller portion of the land. Avon's Resolution 25-20 begins annexation proceedings.

  3. Jul 13, 2026

    The draft Community Housing Plan sets the goal of 300 homes: about 100 first, then about 200 north of the river.

  4. Aug 2026

    Reported Avon signs a contract to buy the land from the State Land Board. Press reports say the terms require all residential development to be deed-restricted community housing.

  5. Next

    Closing, annexation, zoning, choice of development partner, design and tenure. None of these is confirmed here.

04 · The EpiVail approach

A story worth telling,
and a plan to fill it.

What EpiVail would bring to the project's marketing and representation, once the owner chooses to engage it. These are proposed services, not current work.

  1. 01

    A name and an identity

    Place branding grounded in the river and the valley: naming options, a visual identity and a voice, all for the owner to approve. Nothing launches under a name the Town has not chosen.

  2. 02

    A digital home

    A dedicated site, as considered as this one, that answers the real questions: where it is, what it costs, who qualifies and what happens next, kept current as decisions are made.

  3. 03

    An interest list, followed up

    Once authorized: interest capture and CRM follow-up so that no inquiry goes cold, with dated next steps and honest updates while approvals move forward.

  4. 04

    Clarity on eligibility and availability

    Plain-language guides to income limits, deed restrictions, the application or lottery process, and lease-up or sales timing. Clear answers build trust, and trust fills homes.

  5. 05

    Local outreach

    Direct work with the valley's employers, schools, hospitals and service organizations, so the households the Town intends to serve hear about it first.

  6. 06

    A repeatable playbook

    Avon plans more than a thousand community homes over ten years. A launch built well here becomes a template for the Town's next one.

Fair questions

Is this the official Dowd Junction project website?

No. It is a private concept from EpiVail, made to open a conversation with the project's decision-makers. No developer, name, design or brokerage has been appointed for this concept, and none is implied.

Are these photographs of the site?

The aerial view shows the actual corridor. The ground-level photographs show the Eagle River, Minturn, EagleVail and Avon nearby, and each caption says where it was taken. There are no renderings, because no design has been approved.

Who is developing it?

That has not been decided in any document we found. A Corum Development sign has been seen near the site, and a January 2026 Town of Vail report notes Corum using off-site storage at Dowd Junction for its separate Southface Vail project. Neither establishes a role here.

Can I sign up or apply?

Not yet. There are no homes, prices or applications to offer. This page collects no information.
