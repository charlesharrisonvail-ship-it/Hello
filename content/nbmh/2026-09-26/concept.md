# 2026-09-26 — "Low Sun"

**Topic** Seasonal light change and what it does to sleep, energy, and focus.
**Objective** Educate, and make a timely reason to ask about Medication Management.
**Search intent** Medication Management Colorado · Colorado mental health telehealth · Eagle County.
**CTA** Visit NBMentalHealth.com or call 970-470-2939 with questions about
Medication Management or telehealth availability.

## Visual concept

An editorial light study. One idea, one headline, one gesture: a late-autumn sun
sitting low and cropped by the bottom of the frame, with the copy held in open
daylight above it and a deep green horizon band carrying the service line and
contact details. Wholly abstract and typographic — no stock scene, no figures,
no clinical imagery.

Deliberately unlike the September 10 post, which was a photographic still life
(glass sphere on folded paper) with the contact bar floating on the image.

Built in code — no Higgsfield spend. Standing Higgsfield days are Tuesday and
Friday.

## Compliance

`scripts/compliance-check.py` returns PASS on the caption and on the graphic
copy. Checked by hand as well:

- No prohibited terminology; no age range; no Psychiatric Diagnostic Evaluations.
- No diagnosis is named, so the seasonal angle stays general education and makes
  no clinical claim.
- No prescription promise, dosage, individualized advice, or guaranteed outcome.
- No PHI, patient story, or testimonial.
- Official logo used unchanged, small and unobtrusive.
- Coverage sits above the contact CTA in the caption.
- 6 hashtags.
- 1080 × 1350 JPEG, asserted by the renderer.
- No motif that reads as a chart, and so no implied statistic.
