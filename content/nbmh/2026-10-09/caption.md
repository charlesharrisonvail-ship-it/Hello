Not sure what to expect from a first Medication Management visit? It's simply a conversation.

At New Beginnings Mental Health, your first visit is a chance to talk through what you've been noticing — sleep, focus, energy, mood — along with any medications you've tried before and how they worked for you. Together, we talk through your goals and the options that may fit, and you leave with a clear sense of next steps.

It all happens by telehealth, from wherever you are in Colorado.

Bring your questions. New Beginnings is ready to listen.

We're in-network with Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Mountain Strong EAP, and Olivia's Fund. Private-pay appointments are also available.
