Small changes are worth noticing.

When you're working on a medication plan, the details you notice between visits matter — how you're sleeping, your energy and appetite, your focus and mood, and anything that feels different. A quick note on your phone or in a notebook makes it easier to remember at your next appointment.

At New Beginnings Mental Health, those notes help shape the conversation in your follow-up visits, so your plan can keep pace with how you're actually feeling. If something concerns you, bring it up rather than adjusting things on your own — that's what follow-up is for.
