## Voice Patterns Extracted

**Openings — he never opens with himself.** Three of four start with the audience's situation or a story: a pain question ("Struggling to find…"), an anecdote ("Walking my dog tonight…"), or a market claim ("Real estate cycles are usually predictable, but…"). The one that does introduce him puts it in line two, after the hook.

**The analogy is the argument.** Brain surgery / white coat. "Out of orbit." "Structural standoff." Charles names a thing and the naming does the persuading — he rarely argues point-by-point.

**Short verdicts as punctuation.** "No thank you." "Expect the unexpected." Dropped in after a longer passage to land it.

**Credential stacking is compressed, never bragged.** Everything in one breath: "15 years of experience, certified as an RSPS and MRP, and leading the area for the first ever AI powered real estate brokerage." Stated as fact and moved past — no dwelling.

**Qualify the audience out loud.** "If you're a cash buyer looking to move quickly and smart…" — he names who the message is for rather than pitching everyone.

**Contrast pairs.** Cutting-edge tech *and* deep local know-how. Industry knowledge *vs.* AI hype. He consistently positions at the intersection of two things rather than on one axis.

**Every piece signs off with the tagline.** Always. Varying the wrap: "Scale smarter, build better, Epique Mountain Collective" (buyer content) vs. "Scale Smarter. Build Better. EpiVail." (agent/market content). The brand named last is the one the piece is recruiting for.

**Register shifts with the job:**

| Job | Person | Type | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sell to buyers | First person, on camera | Sans | Warm, direct, credentialed |
| Attract agents | First person, story | Sans | Conversational, dry humor, peer-to-peer |
| Market commentary | No person, no face | Serif | Analyst, measured, authoritative |
| International | First person, localized | Sans | Same warmth, fully translated including tagline |

**Production signature:** ~30–35 seconds. 1080×1920 vertical. Word-level captions, 2–4 words at a time, with a keyword in accent color. QR code persistent on buyer content. Hard CTA card at the end of recruitment content (APPLY NOW + charlesharrison@epique.me).
