Score them as a set with the rubric. Six criteria, each zero, one or two. Identity: is it recognizably the same spokesperson in all three? Voice: authorized, and saying names and numbers right? Accuracy: every fact matched to a source you recorded? Pacing: hook in about three seconds, fifteen to thirty seconds long, one point? Captions and audio: captions match speech, and speech is clear on a phone speaker? CTA and disclosure: one clear call to action and clear AI identification in every video?

The proposed pass is ten out of twelve, with no critical failure. Two things are critical: an unauthorized likeness or voice, and a material factual error. Either one means not yet, whatever the score.
