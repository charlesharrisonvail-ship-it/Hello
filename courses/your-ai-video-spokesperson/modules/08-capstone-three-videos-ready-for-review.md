# Capstone — Three Videos Ready for Review

**Course:** Your AI Video Spokesperson · **Status:** draft manuscript · **Video:** not yet recorded

## 1. Objective and required inputs

Produce three finished short videos, each a separate 9:16 export, that are ready for
**your** review before publishing:

1. **Introduction** — the spokesperson/agent introduction, correctly identifying AI use.
2. **Educational point** — one useful real estate point using verified information.
3. **Invitation** — to learn about a fictional practice listing, or a real listing you're
   authorized to market.

**You need:** everything from Modules 1–7 and `resources/capstone-rubric.md`.

**Self-assessed.** No instructor reviews your videos. Nothing is published
automatically; you decide whether and when to post.

## 2. Narrated script

*Narrator: Eva Vail, AiRE Estate Academy's AI presenter, using owner-approved Eva assets and voice. Target read time about 3 minutes.*

Three videos. That's the capstone. Not one great one; three that hold together as the
same spokesperson, saying accurate things, clearly, with a clear next step.

Score them as a set with the rubric. Six criteria, each zero, one or two. Identity: is
it recognizably the same spokesperson in all three? Voice: authorized, and saying names
and numbers right? Accuracy: every fact matched to a source you recorded? Pacing: hook
in about three seconds, fifteen to thirty seconds long, one point? Captions and audio:
captions match speech, and speech is clear on a phone speaker? CTA and disclosure: one
clear call to action and clear AI identification in every video?

The proposed pass is ten out of twelve, with no critical failure. Two things are
critical: an unauthorized likeness or voice, and a material factual error. Either one
means not yet, whatever the score.

This is a self-assessment. I'm not reviewing your work, and nothing gets posted for you.
The rubric tells you exactly what ready looks like, and the remediation table tells you
which module to revisit if you're not there.

When you're ready, record your completion. Then publish if and when you choose, with
the same review you'd give anything else that carries your name.

## 4. Walkthrough

1. Finish the three videos (Modules 4–6).
2. Watch them back to back on your phone, sound off then on.
3. Score each rubric criterion for the set; write one line of evidence each.
4. Check for critical failures.
5. If 10+ with no critical failure, record completion. Otherwise remediate and rescore.
