# Captions and Audio Checklist

Most people watch short videos on a phone, often with the sound off. Captions carry the
message; audio must still be clear when the sound is on.

**Video:** ______________________ **Date:** ____________

## Captions

- [ ] Captions are on and burned in or attached, as the platform needs.
- [ ] Every caption matches what's spoken, word for word where it matters (names, numbers,
      dates, addresses).
- [ ] Automated transcription corrected by hand (check: brokerage name, street names, prices).
- [ ] Bold, high-contrast font; large enough to read on a phone at arm's length.
- [ ] Placed inside the safe area: not under the platform's buttons, profile, or caption
      text at the bottom and right edge.
- [ ] No more than two lines on screen at once.
- [ ] Editable text kept in a project file so you can fix a typo without re-rendering, where
      your editor allows.

## Audio

- [ ] Speech first: voice is clearly louder than music at every moment.
- [ ] Music (if any) is licensed for commercial social use.
- [ ] No clipping or distortion on loud words.
- [ ] Played back on a **phone speaker** at normal volume: every word understandable.
- [ ] Played back with headphones: no hum, echo or abrupt cuts.

## Export

- [ ] Separate 9:16 file for each video (no merged grid of several videos).
- [ ] A clean version (no captions/music) kept where practical, for future edits.
- [ ] File names: `[date]_[topic]_9x16_captioned.mp4` and `_clean.mp4`.
- [ ] "Made with AI" label visible.

## Facts in captions

- [ ] Captions don't add any fact the voice didn't say.
- [ ] Numbers in captions match the source.
