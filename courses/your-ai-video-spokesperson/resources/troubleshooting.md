# Troubleshooting — Your AI Video Spokesperson

Provider details checked October 23, 2026 against HeyGen's help center. Features, labels
and limits change; check help.heygen.com when your screen differs. We teach real estate
use, not software support.

## The avatar doesn't look like me / looks "off"

- Re-check the source footage: HeyGen recommends at least 1080p at 30 fps, bright even
  lighting, a simple static background, eye contact with the lens, and one continuous
  recording with no cuts. Whatever is in your footage (a cough, an odd movement, echo)
  can show up in the avatar.
- HeyGen's help center says each avatar slot can be recreated once per billing cycle
  (month), and deleting an avatar removes all its looks. Fix the footage before you redo it.

## Lip sync is poor

- Make sure your original footage includes you speaking. HeyGen's guidance says this matters
  for lip sync even if you later use a different voice.
- Shorten long sentences; very long run-ons can drift.
- Preview a short section before rendering the full video.

## A name or place is mispronounced

- Use your Pronunciation Sheet. Respell the word for the voice in the script (e.g.,
  "Herron" for "Heron") and keep the correct spelling in the captions.
- Test with a 10-second preview, not a full render.

## The look changed between videos

- You probably changed more than one thing (wardrobe + background + framing). Go back to
  the master look and change one element at a time.

## The video walks or gestures strangely

- Prefer a natural, stationary presentation. Use cuts and B-roll for movement instead of
  asking the avatar to walk.

## Captions are wrong

- Automated transcription misses names and numbers. Correct every caption by hand and
  check against the script and the source.

## Music drowns out the voice

- Lower the music until speech is clearly on top; test on a phone speaker.

## I keep re-rendering and spending credits

- Stop after two attempts at the same problem. Diagnose with this page and the pre-render
  checklist. Check the provider's current pricing before rendering again.

## Consent video was rejected

- Read the provider's exact consent text, keep it under 30 seconds (HeyGen), use clear
  light and sound, and make sure it's the same person as the footage.

## Someone asks me to make an avatar of them

- They must record their own consent with the provider, and you should have a written
  agreement about use. Don't clone anyone from public photos or videos.
