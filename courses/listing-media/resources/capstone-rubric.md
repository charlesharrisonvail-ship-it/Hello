# Capstone Rubric — Listing Media Without the Invoice

**This is a self-assessment.** No instructor reviews your work. Score yourself honestly
against the criteria below; the rubric exists so you can see exactly what "premium and
honest" means before you start. It is a private certificate of completion, not a
continuing-education credit or an association designation.

## What you build

One property you have the right to market: your own listing (with the seller's permission),
a friend's home with their permission, or your own house.

1. A drone-style aerial clip from one exterior photograph.
2. A Listing Showcase: a room-by-room walkthrough from photos you already own.
3. A month of social posts, drafted in one sitting, in your voice.
4. One "getting found" update: your Google Business Profile, or an answer-first post that
   AI assistants can quote.

## Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2

| Criterion | 0 — Not yet | 1 — Partly | 2 — Premium and honest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aerial clip** | Warped buildings, invented features, or no AI label | Smooth, but a detail of the home or setting changed | Smooth flight; the home and setting look as they are; labeled "Made with AI" |
| **Listing Showcase** | Shows rooms or features the home doesn't have | Accurate, but pacing or captions are rough | Every room is the real room; clean movement and captions; labeled as AI-made |
| **Social month** | Generic posts, or a post that fails your fair-housing read | Your voice in most posts; a few mix audiences | A month in your voice, one audience per post, every claim true |
| **Getting found** | Nothing updated, or claims without sources | Updated, but with stock photos or vague answers | Real photos of real properties; an answer-first post with numbers and named sources |
| **Accuracy and disclosure** | A fact contradicts the listing, or brokerage details are missing | Facts match; one AI visual isn't labeled | Every fact matches the listing; every AI visual labeled; brokerage rules followed |

**Total:** ____ / 10

## Passing

- **Passing score: 8 out of 10**, with **no critical failure**.
- **Critical failures** (any one means not yet, whatever the score):
  - An AI visual misrepresents the home's condition, size or features.
  - Photos or a property used without the owner's or seller's permission.
  - Anything that fails your fair-housing read.

## If you're not there yet

| Weak spot | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Aerial clip | Start from a sharper photo and keep the camera move simple. Revisit Module 4. |
| Listing Showcase | Use only real room photos; slow the movement. Revisit Module 5. |
| Social month | Rewrite with one audience per post, in your own words. Revisit Module 6. |
| Getting found | Swap in real photos; lead each answer with the facts and name sources. Revisit Module 7. |
| Accuracy and disclosure | Check every fact against the listing; add the AI label everywhere. |
| Critical failure | Fix the cause, then rescore all four pieces. |
