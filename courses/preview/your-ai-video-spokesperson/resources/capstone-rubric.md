# Capstone Rubric — Three Videos Ready for Review

**Self-assessment.** No instructor reviews your videos, and nothing is published
automatically. You score your own work and decide when to publish. Your certificate is a
private certificate of completion, not a continuing-education credit or an association
designation.

## The three videos

1. **Introduction** — the spokesperson or agent introduction, clearly identifying AI use.
2. **Educational point** — one useful real estate point using verified information.
3. **Invitation** — invitation to learn about a fictional practice listing, or a real
   listing you're authorized to market.

Each as a separate 9:16 export, captioned, with a clean version where practical.

## Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2 (score the set; a weak video lowers the score)

| Criterion | 0 — Not yet | 1 — Partly | 2 — Ready for review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Identity** | Look changes noticeably between videos or has visible artifacts | Mostly consistent; one video drifts | Same recognizable spokesperson in all three; variations are planned |
| **Voice** | Unauthorized or mismatched voice | Authorized voice; a mispronunciation remains | Authorized voice; names, places and numbers pronounced correctly |
| **Accuracy** | A material factual error, or an unsourced claim | Facts correct but sources not recorded | Every fact matches a recorded source |
| **Pacing** | Hook after 5 s, or over 45 s with filler | Clear but slow start or rushed end | Hook in about 3 s; 15–30 s; one point |
| **Captions/audio** | Captions wrong or missing; music covers speech | Minor caption errors or level issues | Captions match speech; speech clear on a phone speaker |
| **CTA/disclosure** | No CTA, or AI use not identified | One of the two is weak | One clear CTA and clear AI identification in each video |

**Total:** ____ / 12

## Passing

- **Proposed pass: 10 of 12**, with **no critical failure**.
- **Critical failures** (any one means not yet):
  - An unauthorized likeness or voice (anyone who isn't you or a properly licensed
    fictional character, including AiRE's instructors).
  - A material factual error (wrong price, address, date, bedroom count, or a claim you
    can't source).

## Remediation

| Weak spot | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Return to the master look; change one element at a time (Module 2). |
| Voice | Pronunciation test before rendering (Module 3). Confirm voice authorization. |
| Accuracy | Check each fact against the listing sheet or an official source; record it (Module 4). |
| Pacing | Cut the first sentence; time the script aloud (Module 4). |
| Captions/audio | Correct captions by hand; lower music; phone-speaker test (Module 6). |
| CTA/disclosure | One CTA; add the AI label and disclosure line (Modules 1 and 4). |
