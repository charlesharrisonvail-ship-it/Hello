# Likeness and Voice Rights Checklist

Education, not legal advice. Laws on likeness, voice and AI disclosure differ by state
and change. If you're unsure, ask an attorney before you publish.

Provider requirements checked October 23, 2026 against HeyGen's help center
("Recording your Consent Video," "Digital Twin FAQ"). Re-check before you record.

## A. Own-likeness avatar

- [ ] The person in the footage is me.
- [ ] I will record the provider's consent video myself, reading the provider's exact
      consent text. (HeyGen: under 30 seconds, same person as the footage, submitted with
      the footage.)
- [ ] My footage is my own recording, not a clip from someone else's video or another AI tool.
      (HeyGen's FAQ says it doesn't support someone else's videos without permission or
      content generated by third-party AI platforms.)
- [ ] My voice clone (if any) is my voice, recorded by me.
- [ ] I've read the provider's terms on who owns the avatar, how it's stored, and how to
      delete it.
- [ ] My brokerage allows AI avatar video in marketing (ask if unsure).

## B. Fictional spokesperson

- [ ] The character is not based on, and does not resemble on purpose, a real person.
- [ ] I created the character's look with a tool whose terms allow commercial use, or I
      licensed a stock avatar whose license covers my use.
- [ ] The voice is a stock/licensed voice whose terms allow commercial use, or a voice I
      have written rights to.
- [ ] The character never claims to be a licensed agent or to have personal experiences.
- [ ] Every video discloses that the spokesperson is AI-generated.

## C. Never

- [ ] I am not cloning anyone else's face or voice, including a colleague, a client, a
      family member, or a public figure.
- [ ] I am not using AiRE's instructors (Eva Vail, or AiRE's narrator) or AiRE Estate's
      owner as my spokesperson or voice. The course demonstrates with them; it grants you
      no rights to copy them.
- [ ] I am not using a voice that "sounds like" a real person to imply they said something.
