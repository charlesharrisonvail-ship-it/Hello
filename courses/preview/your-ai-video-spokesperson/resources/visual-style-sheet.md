# Visual Style Sheet

Consistency is what makes a spokesperson recognizable. Lock the master look; vary one
thing at a time.

HeyGen filming guidance (checked Oct. 23, 2026): bright, even lighting; simple static
background; avoid clothing with large logos or text; glasses and large accessories may
create minor artifacts.

## 4. Don'ts

- Don't change wardrobe, hair and background in the same new look.
- Don't use backgrounds with readable signs, other people, or other brokerages' signs.
- Don't put text where captions or the platform's buttons will cover it.
