Fill it in before you open any video tool.

## 1. Route

☐ **Own likeness.** An avatar of me, from my own footage and my own voice.
☐ **Fictional spokesperson.** A character that is not a real person, created or licensed
  so I have the right to use it commercially.

Never: a real person who isn't you (a colleague, a celebrity, a client, a family member)
unless that person records their own consent with the provider and signs a written
agreement with you. Publicly available photos or clips are not permission.
