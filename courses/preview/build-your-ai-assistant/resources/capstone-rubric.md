# Capstone Rubric — Your Assistant, Working

**This is a self-assessment.** No instructor reviews your submission. Score yourself
honestly against the criteria below; the rubric exists so you can see exactly what
"working" means. Your certificate of completion depends on the module checks, and on
you recording that you finished the capstone. It is a private certificate of completion,
not a continuing-education credit or an association designation.

## What you assemble

1. Your Assistant Job Card.
2. Your master instructions (or skill file).
3. Your sanitized reference pack (fictional data or real data with personal details removed).
4. Three sample outputs from three different properties.
5. Your completed ten-case test log, with observed results and corrections.

## Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2

| Criterion | 0 — Not yet | 1 — Partly | 2 — Working |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Task clarity** | The job is broad ("help with sellers") or missing | One job, but inputs or outputs are vague | One job in one sentence; inputs, output and acceptance criteria are written down |
| **Source fidelity** | Any draft contains a number or claim not in the inputs | All facts come from inputs, but sources aren't listed | Every fact is traceable to a listed source; missing and stale items are flagged |
| **Voice** | Drafts read as generic AI text | Some of your phrasing, inconsistent across drafts | All three drafts sound like you, without adding claims or hype |
| **Boundaries** | The assistant gives pricing advice, legal interpretation, or offers to send | Boundaries are written but one draft crosses them | No draft crosses a boundary; decisions are flagged for the agent |
| **Repeatability** | Works only with hand-holding in one chat | Works again but needs re-explaining | Saved as Project instructions or a skill; three properties run with the same setup |

## Passing

- **Proposed pass: 8 out of 10**, with **no critical failure**.
- **Critical failures** (any one means not yet, whatever the score):
  - A draft states a fact that is not in the inputs (fabricated fact).
  - A draft includes a buyer's or other client's personal information.
  - The setup can send, post or contact anyone without your review.

## If you're not there yet

| Weak spot | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Task clarity | Rewrite the Job Card sentence until it names one input and one output. Re-watch Module 1. |
| Source fidelity | Add "list each fact with its source in Part 2" to your output format. Rerun T01–T07. |
| Voice | Replace adjectives in your Voice Profile with real samples. Re-watch Module 2. |
| Boundaries | Add the escalation lines from the master instructions. Rerun T03, T04, T06. |
| Repeatability | Move instructions into a Project or a skill file. Re-watch Module 4 or 5. |
| Critical failure | Fix the cause, rerun the full ten-case test, and record the rerun date. |
