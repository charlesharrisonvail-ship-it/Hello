## 6. Rules that beat voice

These win every time, even if the voice would say otherwise:

1. Facts come only from the supplied sources. Voice never adds a fact.
2. Missing facts stay missing and are flagged.
3. No language about protected classes, school quality claims, or "neighborhood type."
4. No promises about price, timing, or results.
