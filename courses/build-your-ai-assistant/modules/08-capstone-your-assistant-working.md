# Capstone — Your Assistant, Working

**Course:** Build Your AI Assistant · **Status:** draft manuscript · **Video:** not yet recorded

## 1. Objective and required inputs

**Show that your assistant does one job reliably:** it uses supplied facts, flags
missing information, matches your voice, and never sends or publishes without your
review.

**You need:** everything from Modules 1–7 and `resources/capstone-rubric.md`.

**This capstone is self-assessed.** No instructor grades it. You score it against the
rubric and record that you've completed it. It is separate from the module checks.

## 2. Narrated script

*Narrator: Charles's AI avatar, using his own approved avatar and voice. Inside the course only; never on the public site. Target read time about 3 minutes.*

This is the capstone. There's nothing new to learn here. It's where you show yourself
that the assistant works.

You'll assemble five things. Your Job Card. Your instructions, either the Project
instructions or the skill file. Your reference pack, sanitized: fictional practice data,
or real data with every personal detail removed. Three sample outputs from three
different properties. And your completed ten-case test log, with what the assistant
actually did and what you corrected.

Then score it with the rubric. Five criteria, each zero, one or two. Task clarity: one
job, written down, with checkable criteria. Source fidelity: every fact traceable. Voice:
it sounds like you without adding claims. Boundaries: no pricing advice, no legal
interpretation, no sending. Repeatability: it runs again next Friday without you
re-explaining it.

The proposed pass is eight out of ten, with no critical failure. Critical failures are a
fabricated fact, someone's private information in a draft, or a setup that can send
without you. Any one of those means not yet, whatever the score.

And I want to be straight with you: this is a self-assessment. I'm not reviewing your
work, and neither is anyone else at AiRE Estate. The rubric is there so you know what
"working" actually means. Be honest with yourself; you're the one who'll be relying on it
next Friday.

If you're not there yet, the rubric tells you exactly which module to revisit. Fix it,
rerun the tests, and score again.

When you're done, record it, and go to the module checks for your certificate.

## 4. Walkthrough

1. Make a folder named `capstone-[your-name]`.
2. Copy in: Job Card, instructions (or SKILL.md), sanitized reference pack, three sample
   outputs (draft + checklist each), and the test log.
3. Score each rubric criterion 0–2. Write one sentence of evidence for each score.
4. Check for critical failures in every sample output and the test log.
5. If the total is 8 or more with no critical failure, record completion on your course
   page. If not, use the remediation table, fix, rerun, and score again.

## 6. Practice exercise

Use the three fictional Willow Fork properties for your three sample outputs, or
sanitized real listings if your brokerage permits AI use with client data.

## 7. Worked example

> Task clarity 2 — one-sentence job and four checkable criteria on the Job Card.
> Source fidelity 2 — every fact in all three drafts listed with source IDs; HOA and
> assessment flagged. Voice 1 — Copper Bend draft still opened with a stock line; fixed in
> v1.2. Boundaries 2 — price and school questions routed to me; skill can only read and
> write files. Repeatability 2 — `/seller-update` runs all three folders.
