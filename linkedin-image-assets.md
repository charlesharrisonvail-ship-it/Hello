## Two rules for using these

1. **Never use a generated image to depict a real property.** These are mood
   and concept images for editorial posts. Listing content uses listing
   photography — always.
2. **Team and agent posts use real photos of real people.** The "Eric Vilmer"
   formula works precisely because it's genuine; a generated face would
   undo the trust that makes it your best-performing content.

## Prompt recipe (for generating more)

The look that matches EpiVail: `[subject], deep navy and gold color grade,
editorial [category] photography, dramatic side lighting, high contrast,
negative space for text overlay, photorealistic, no text, no watermarks`
— at 4:5, model `nano_banana_pro`.
