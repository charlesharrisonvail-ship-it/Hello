## The Honest Math First

10,000 organic followers is a real campaign, not a hack. On LinkedIn, an
optimized profile converts roughly **1 new follower per 200–400 post
impressions**. That means 10K followers requires on the order of **2–4 million
cumulative impressions** — which is absolutely achievable in 6–9 months with
the system below, because LinkedIn growth compounds: every follower gained
raises the baseline reach of the next post.

| Phase | Timeline | Target | Engine |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | Weeks 1–2 | Profile converts 3–5× better | Profile rebuild + Featured section |
| 2. Ignition | Weeks 3–8 | +1,000–1,500 followers | 5 posts/wk + 15 comments/day |
| 3. Momentum | Months 3–5 | +3,500–4,500 followers | Double down on winners (data loop) |
| 4. Compounding | Months 6–9 | +4,000–5,000 followers | Video + cross-platform + collabs |

Two audiences, scored separately (they behave differently):
1. **Agent attraction** (primary) — real estate agents in the US, France,
   Australia, Mexico considering Epique.
2. **Luxury Resimercial™** (secondary) — buyers, sellers, and investors in the
   Vail–Beaver Creek corridor.

## Phase 1 — Profile as Landing Page (Weeks 1–2)

Every post you write sends people to your profile. If it doesn't convert
visitors to followers, everything downstream leaks. Fix these in order:

1. **Follow, not Connect.** Turn on Creator mode so your primary button is
   "Follow" — connection requests cap at 30K; follows don't, and follows are
   the metric we're growing.
2. **Headline** (the single highest-leverage line — it travels with every
   comment you leave): lead with the outcome for the *reader*, not your title.
   Draft: `Helping agents build equity, not just commissions | Luxury
   Resimercial™ in Vail–Beaver Creek | Charles Harrison, Epique Area/Growth
   Leader`
3. **Banner** — navy/gold EpiVail brand: Vail backdrop, one line of
   positioning, one CTA ("Follow for agent growth + mountain luxury").
4. **About section** — first 3 lines are visible before "see more." Open with
   the agent-attraction hook, close with a direct CTA and how to reach you.
5. **Featured section** — pin 3 items: (a) your best agent-attraction post,
   (b) the EpiVail agent page (routes into Lofty), (c) one Vail market proof
   piece. Refresh monthly with whatever's winning.
6. **Custom URL + contact info** — clean vanity URL; make sure email is
   visible to followers.

## Phase 2 — The Content Engine (Weeks 3–8)

### Cadence
**5 posts per week** (Mon–Fri). Consistency beats intensity — the algorithm
rewards accounts that show up daily, and 5/week is sustainable indefinitely.

### Pillar mix
| Pillar | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Agent attraction / agent growth | 50% | Recruits agents; your highest-intent audience |
| Luxury Resimercial™ / Vail market | 30% | Authority + local dominance; most shareable |
| Personal story / behind-the-scenes | 20% | The follow-trigger — people follow people |

### Formats that grow followers (in order)
1. **Text + single image** — fastest to produce, strong baseline reach.
2. **Carousels (PDF docs)** — highest saves/shares; use for "how-to" agent
   content and market breakdowns.
3. **Short vertical video** — repurpose TikTok (@epivail) winners via the
   Remotion pipeline; native upload, captions on.
4. **Polls** — sparingly (1–2/month) for reach spikes and market intel.

### Hook formulas (first line decides everything)
- Contrarian: "Most agents don't have a production problem. They have an equity problem."
- Number + stakes: "3 things I'd tell any agent earning under $150K in a resort market."
- Story cold-open: "An agent called me from France last week with one question."
- Local authority: "What $4M actually buys you in Beaver Creek right now."

### Rules
- First line ≤ 8 words when possible. No "I'm excited to announce."
- One idea per post. End with a question or a soft CTA to follow.
- No external links in the post body (kills reach) — links go in comments.
- Byline everywhere it appears: **Charles Harrison, Epique Area/Growth Leader** — exactly that, no regional descriptor.

## Distribution Loops (this is where 10K actually comes from)

Posting alone won't get there. These three loops multiply every post:

### Loop 1 — Strategic commenting (15/day, ~30 min)
Comment early and substantively on 15 posts/day from: large real-estate
creators, Epique leadership, luxury market voices, and agents in your four
target countries. A great comment on a 100K-follower account puts your
headline in front of thousands. This is the single fastest cold-start tactic —
many creators attribute their first 5K followers to comments, not posts.

### Loop 2 — Cross-platform funnel
Every LinkedIn winner becomes a TikTok (@epivail) script and a Facebook
(@CharlesHarrisonVail) post; every TikTok winner becomes a LinkedIn vertical
video. One idea, three surfaces. Route replies and leads into Lofty per the
existing workflow.

### Loop 3 — DM + engagement follow-through
- Reply to **every** comment on your posts within 2 hours (doubles the
  post's second-wave reach).
- When an agent engages twice, send a short, human DM — no pitch, just open
  the conversation. This converts followers into recruitment pipeline, which
  is the actual point of the 10K.

**What we will NOT do:** engagement pods, follow/unfollow churn, bought
followers, or automation that violates LinkedIn ToS. They inflate the number
and poison the reach of the account we're trying to build.

## The Measurement Loop (monthly)

This plan gets sharper every 30 days with real data:

1. LinkedIn profile → **Show all analytics** → note baseline: profile views,
   post impressions, engagements, new followers (last 30 days).
2. **Export the full CSV** (Analytics → Post analytics → Export; choose the
   longest date range available, not a snippet).
3. Open in **Google Sheets**, keep: date, impressions, engagement rate,
   profile views, new followers, post topic, post format.
4. Send the CSV to Claude → get: best days, winning topics, format
   performance, agent-attraction vs. market-content split — and a refreshed
   2-week calendar that doubles down on what's working.

**KPIs per 30-day cycle:** new followers, followers-per-1K-impressions
(conversion), profile-view→follow rate, and DM conversations started with
agents (the metric that pays for all of this).
