**Charles Harrison, Epique Area/Growth Leader** · Source: LinkedIn aggregate
analytics export, full 365-day range.

## Headline Insight

**Your content quality is fine. Your volume and distribution are the problem.**
Engagement rate is 2.41% — right at the LinkedIn healthy benchmark — but the
whole year produced only 5,733 impressions (~16/day) and **196 new followers**.
At the current pace, 10,000 new followers takes ~50 years. The fix is not
better posts; it's 5× more posts, a daily commenting loop, and reframed
agent-attraction content. The audience you want is *already following you* —
see Demographics below.

## Finding 1 — Tuesday and Monday are your proven days

Tuesday drove 1,378 impressions (24% of the year) and Monday 1,068 (19%) —
together **43% of all reach**. Six of your top ten days were a Monday or
Tuesday, including both breakout days (Jan 20: 265 imp; Mar 17: 258 imp).
Wed–Sun are statistically flat (600–720 each). → Anchor the heaviest content
on Mon/Tue; never waste a big post on a weekend.

## Finding 2 — Momentum peaked in Oct–Nov 2025 and has been fading since

New followers by month: Oct 26 → **Nov 41** → then a steady slide to 8 (May),
8 (Jun), 5 (Jul). The Oct–Nov peak coincided with your most *human* content:
the "Welcome Eric Vilmer to the team" post (#1 engagement of the year, 9 eng /
118 imp), the Luxury Resimercial™ commercial-residential listing (Oct 20 —
your two best follower days, 5+5, were Oct 20–21), and Vail snow-report
lifestyle posts. The last three months are your worst of the year. The engine
that worked has simply stopped running.
