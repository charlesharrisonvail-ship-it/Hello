# Vail Market Snapshot — August 2026

Source: Vail MLS, residential (property type A), pulled 2026-09-17.
Prior snapshot (July) is the basis of `assets/carousel-vail-market-aug2026.pdf`.

## The month-over-month move

| Metric | July 2026 | **Aug 2026** | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active listings | 191 | **188** | Plateaued at the high |
| New listings | 36 | **27** | Sellers pulling back (66 in June) |
| Closed sales | 19 | **19** | Flat |
| Median sold | $2,225,000 | **$1,975,000** | Down $250K |
| Median ask (active) | $2,990,000 | **$2,970,000** | Barely moved |
| **Ask-vs-sold gap** | $772K (26%) | **$995K (33%)** | **Widened sharply** |
| Avg days on market | 103.4 | **118.3** | 5th straight monthly rise |
| Avg sold price | $4,770,250 | **$3,279,169** | Fewer mega-sales in Aug |

## The story for the next market post

**The August carousel's central claim got stronger, not weaker.** That post
said sellers were asking ~$772K more than buyers were paying. One month later
that gap is **just under $1 million** — roughly a third above what homes
actually close at.

Three angles, strongest first:

1. **"The gap hit $1M."** Direct sequel to the August post, and the number is
   dramatic on its own. Sellers barely moved their asking prices ($2.99M →
   $2.97M) while closings dropped $250K. That's the entire story in two rows.
2. **New listings collapsed — 66 in June to 27 in August.** Sellers are
   quietly withdrawing rather than repricing. That's a leading indicator worth
   naming before anyone else does.
3. **Days on market has climbed five months straight** — 60 in April to 118 in
   August, nearly double. Pair with #1: the gap is *why* the clock is running.

## Caveats to keep the numbers honest

- Monthly medians in a market this thin (11–28 sales/month) swing hard. The
  **trend** in the gap and in DOM is the signal; any single month's median is
  not.
- Average sold price is dominated by a handful of ultra-luxury closings — it
  moved $1.5M in one month on 19 sales. Use median for "what's happening,"
  average only to make the two-markets point.
- Months of supply is still ~10 (188 active ÷ 19 closings), unchanged and
  still well past the six-month buyer's-market line.
