AiRE assistant and optional email collection

AiRE is an AI assistant that answers course questions using approved AiRE Estate website information. Questions you submit are processed by Cloudflare Workers and Workers AI. Please do not enter access codes, passwords, payment details, private client information or other sensitive information in the chat.

If you request a response, we collect your email address, selected course interest, the time of your request and a record of your consent. We use this information to respond to your inquiry. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to marketing email. Chat questions are not attached to your email submission.

Cloudflare provides the assistant’s hosting, AI processing and D1 database storage. The assistant application does not store chat transcripts or IP addresses. Cloudflare may process technical request information to operate and secure its services; see Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Our database keeps daily usage totals to enforce service limits, without attaching them to a visitor.

Email inquiries stored in the assistant’s database are deleted after 90 days by a daily cleanup task. Copies made when we respond by email are handled under the other retention terms in this policy. You may request access, correction or deletion of your information by emailing hello@aireestate.com.
