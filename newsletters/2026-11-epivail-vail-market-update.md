# EpiVail Updates — November 2026

**Vail Heads Into Ski Season: What October's Numbers Tell Us**

Good morning, friends, and happy almost-opening-day.

The lifts are about to turn, the village lights are going up, and folks have
been kind enough to ask me how the Vail market is holding up. So here's the
honest picture, straight from the MLS, without any sugar on it.

## The short version

October was a steady, healthy month in Vail. Homes sold at a good clip, they
sold faster than they did in September, and buyers have more to choose from
than they did a year ago.

| Vail residential, October 2026 | |
|---|---|
| Homes sold | 20 |
| Median sold price | $1,906,250 |
| Average days on market | 83 |
| Active listings at month end | 148 |
| New listings | 21 |

*Source: MLS market statistics via Flexmls, residential, City of Vail,
pulled November 16, 2026.*

## What that means, in plain terms

**Sales held strong.** Twenty closings made October the busiest month for
sales in the past twelve. That's a good sign heading into winter, when a lot of
our buyers are standing in the village and falling for it all over again.

**Homes moved faster.** Average days on market dropped from about 169 in
September to about 83 in October. Well-priced homes are not sitting.

**Buyers have room to breathe.** There were 148 homes for sale at the end of
October, up from 129 a year earlier. Inventory peaked at 191 in July and has
been easing back as the season turns. At October's pace, that's roughly seven
months of supply — a market where a thoughtful buyer can take their time and a
thoughtful seller still gets a fair hearing.

**A word of caution on prices.** With only about 20 sales a month, Vail's
median price swings around quite a bit depending on *which* homes happen to
close. Over the past year it has ranged from about $1.2 million to $2.7
million, so October's $1.9 million says more about the mix of homes that sold
than about values falling. I'd rather tell you that than hand you a headline
that isn't true.
