# EpiVail Updates — November 2026

**Colorado's Market Takes a Breath: What October Means for You**

Good morning, friends, and happy almost-opening-day.

The lifts are about to turn and the first real snow is on the peaks, and many
of you have been kind enough to ask how the Colorado market is holding up. So
here's the honest picture, from the statewide numbers down to our own mountain
valley, without any sugar on it.

## Colorado, statewide

October was a slower month across Colorado. Fewer homes sold, but prices held
nearly steady.

| Colorado, October 2026 | | vs. October 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Homes sold | 6,079 | down 18.6% |
| Median sale price, all homes | $545,000 | down 0.9% |
| Median single-family price | $575,000 | down 0.7% |
| Median condo/townhome price | $415,000 | up 0.2% |

*Source: Colorado Association of REALTORS®, October 2026 market report,
released November 12, 2026.*

In plain terms: buyers across the state are being careful, and they're taking
their time. But sellers aren't slashing prices to get deals done. Values are
holding, and that's the most important thing for anyone who owns a home here.

## Here in the mountains: Eagle County

Our own valley — Vail, Beaver Creek, Avon, Edwards, Eagle and everything
between — followed the same pattern as the rest of the state.

| Eagle County residential, October 2026 | | vs. October 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Homes sold | 97 | down from 116 (−16%) |
| Median sold price | $1,462,500 | down from $1,497,500 (−2%) |
| Active listings at month end | 645 | up from 573 (+13%) |
| Average days on market | 78 | |
| Went under contract | 70 | |

*Source: MLS market statistics via Flexmls, residential, Eagle County, pulled
November 16, 2026.*

**What that means:**

- **Prices held up.** A 2% dip on a mountain median is small, and our monthly
  medians swing with whichever homes happen to close. Over the past year,
  Eagle County's median has ranged from about $1.3 million to $2.0 million.
- **Buyers have more to choose from.** With 645 homes for sale and 97 sales in
  October, we're at roughly six and a half months of supply. That's a balanced
  market. Buyers can take their time, and well-priced homes still sell.
- **Vail itself had a good month.** The Town of Vail closed 20 sales in
  October, its busiest month of the past year. Average time on market fell from
  about 169 days in September to about 83.
