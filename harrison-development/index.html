Services

Five services, one path from concept to closing.

  1. 01

    Market Positioning

    Comparable sales, competitive research, buyer profiles, and pricing recommendations to inform your project’s market strategy. This is marketing research, not an appraisal, engineering or entitlement service.

  2. 02

    Project Branding & Launch

    A cohesive project identity, website, presentation materials, and launch plan built around the property and its intended buyers.

  3. 03

    AI-Enhanced Marketing

    Reviewed visual concepts, video, and campaign content that help communicate the project. Conceptual or AI-generated imagery is always labeled, and never implies unapproved features are actual or approved.

  4. 04

    Buyer Inquiry & Follow-Up

    Inquiry capture, organized follow-up, qualification, appointments, and useful feedback to the development team.

  5. 05

    Licensed Sales Representation

    Showings, negotiations, contracts, and closing coordination through the brokerage arrangement described above. The sales side is what turns the marketing into closings.

Planned community housing on the Eagle River near Avon

Dowd Junction

The public record. Planning documents reviewed in October 2026 describe roughly 100 community homes in a first phase. Whether they will be rentals or for sale, and who will develop them, was not final in the public record.

The concept

  1. Plain-language eligibility guides and one clear interest list, so the people the homes are meant for can find them.
  2. A fair-housing-minded outreach plan in English and Spanish that reaches local employers and the wider eligible community.
  3. Application, lottery and move-in support designed to work for either rentals or sales.

A planned riverfront neighborhood in Eagle

Red Mountain Ranch

The public record. Town filings reviewed in November 2026 describe about 66 homes on the Eagle River. Sale or rental terms have not been published, so this study assumes neither.

The concept

  1. A story and brand direction drawn from the river, the site and the people likely to live there.
  2. Positioning and an interest list that stay accurate whatever the final terms turn out to be.
  3. A launch timeline with inquiry follow-up, plus a 30-second concept reel in which any AI-made visuals are labeled as concept art.

Questions

What developers and landowners ask first.

What does Harrison Development do?

We help developers, builders and landowners position and launch projects, then carry the sales through to closing. That means market research, project branding and launch materials, AI-assisted marketing content, inquiry follow-up, and licensed real estate sales representation.

Are marketing and brokerage services separate?

Yes. The marketing scope is documented separately from brokerage services. Brokerage services and any compensation are covered only by a brokerage agreement through the brokerage. Terms are discussed in a consultation.

Where do you work?

Charles is licensed across the state of Colorado, with a focus on the mountain communities. See the markets above.

How is AI used, and who checks it?

AI helps with production and analysis, such as visual concepts, copy drafts and inquiry patterns. Charles reviews every output. Conceptual or AI-generated imagery is labeled, and never implies that unapproved features are real or approved.

Do you guarantee buyer demand or sales results?

No. We do not promise sales pace, pricing or returns. We aim to give your project a clear story, a defined buyer and consistent follow-up.

I own land but have not decided what to do. Can we talk?

Yes. An initial conversation about your goals, the market context and possible next steps carries no obligation.

What should I send before we talk?

A short description of the project is plenty. Please do not send confidential financial information or sensitive documents. We will tell you what we need once we have spoken.
