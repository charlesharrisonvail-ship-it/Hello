Privacy Notice

Who operates this site

This site is operated by Charles Harrison, doing business as Harrison Development, . Real estate brokerage services are provided through Epique Realty.

What this site collects

This site stores nothing about you. It sets no cookies and loads no advertising pixels or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes standard technical request data (such as your IP address) to deliver and protect the site. The 3D map in the Markets section is provided by Google Maps and loads only when you scroll to it; Google receives standard request data (such as your IP address) and may set its own cookies, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Terms. The site remembers one setting on your own device if you press "Pause motion".

The contact form

When you press the button, the form sends what you typed to this website: your name, email address, project location, project stage and project description, plus any optional details you add (phone number, role, preferred way to connect, and best days or times). It is stored with our website host, Cloudflare, where only Charles Harrison can read it, and it is kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and to discuss marketing and brokerage services. To limit spam, the site briefly keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address for one hour; it is not linked to your inquiry. If the form cannot send, you can email us instead; nothing is saved in that case. Please do not send confidential financial information, sensitive documents, or health information.

The "Choose a time" button opens a Google Calendar booking page, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. If a project conversation leads to brokerage services, information you provide may be shared with the brokerage as needed to serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you sent us by contacting us at the email shown on the main page.
