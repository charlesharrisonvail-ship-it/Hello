Dowd Junction

Planned community housing on the Eagle River near Avon

These are independent concept studies prepared on public information to show how we would approach a launch. Harrison Development and EpiVail are not engaged by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Town of Avon, the Town of Eagle, or any owner, developer, architect or agency, and do not represent this project. No homes are offered for sale or rent here.

Open the full concept page preview

The public record

Reviewed October 2026. Planning documents describe roughly 100 community homes in a first phase. Whether they will be rentals or for sale, and who will develop them, was not final in the public record.

The marketing challenge

Make sure the people the homes are meant for can find them, understand whether they qualify, and apply, in a process that works for either rentals or sales.

Who it is for

Eligible members of the local community and the employers whose staff may qualify, reached in English and Spanish with a fair-housing-minded plan.

The concept

  1. Plain-language eligibility guides and one clear interest list, so the people the homes are meant for can find them.
  2. A fair-housing-minded outreach plan in English and Spanish that reaches local employers and the wider eligible community.
  3. Application, lottery and move-in support designed to work for either rentals or sales.

Proposed deliverables

  • Plain-language eligibility guides
  • One interest list with organized follow-up
  • A bilingual outreach plan (English and Spanish)
  • Application, lottery and move-in support materials

Proposed, not delivered: this is how we would approach the launch, not a record of work done for a client.
