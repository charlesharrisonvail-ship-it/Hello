The public record

Reviewed November 2026. Town filings describe about 66 homes on the Eagle River. Sale or rental terms have not been published, so this study assumes neither.

The marketing challenge

Build a clear story and an interest list early, while the final terms are still unknown, without promising anything the public record does not support.

Who it is for

People who want to live in Eagle, near the river and the town, reached with accurate marketing that works whether the homes end up for sale or for rent.

The concept

  1. A story and brand direction drawn from the river, the site and the people likely to live there.
  2. Positioning and an interest list that stay accurate whatever the final terms turn out to be.
  3. A launch timeline with inquiry follow-up, plus a 30-second concept reel in which any AI-made visuals are labeled as concept art.

Proposed deliverables

  • Project story and brand direction
  • A one-page project site, built for phones
  • A 30-second concept reel
  • An interest list with organized follow-up
  • A launch timeline

Proposed, not delivered: this is how we would approach the launch, not a record of work done for a client.
