# Developer pitch and proposal template (editable)

Draft only. Prices and contract terms are left for negotiation and professional review. No ROI is promised. Nothing here has been sent to anyone.

## Short pitch

> Your project needs a clear story, a defined buyer, and someone who can carry the conversation through to a sale. Harrison Development connects local market knowledge, AI-enhanced marketing, and licensed real estate representation. Let's discuss your project and where marketing and sales support could make a difference.

Sign-off for Harrison Development messages: *Charles Harrison, Harrison Development.* Keep this separate from the EpiVail signature in `CLAUDE.md`. Add the broker-approved attribution line once it exists.

## Proposal outline

1. **Project goals.** What the owner wants from the sale or launch, in their words.
2. **Verified project status.** Entitlements, approvals, ownership and timeline, each with the source document. Mark anything unverified as such.
3. **Market positioning.** Comparable sales, competing supply, buyer profiles, pricing considerations. Not an appraisal.
4. **Deliverables.** Naming/identity, website, presentation materials, launch plan, inquiry handling process. List each with a description.
5. **Client-provided assets and approvals.** Plans, renderings, photos, legal approvals for marketing claims. Who approves what, and by when.
6. **Timeline.** Phases and dates, left for discussion.
7. **Responsibilities.** What Harrison Development does, what the client does, what third parties do.
8. **Marketing budget.** [To be negotiated.] Third-party costs listed separately from any fee.
9. **Reporting.** What is reported (inquiries, showings, buyer feedback) and how often.
10. **Separate brokerage agreement reference.** Brokerage services and compensation are covered only by a separate listing or brokerage agreement through the brokerage. [Reference to be added after broker review.]
11. **Exclusions and unresolved details.** No guarantee of demand, pace or price; no construction, entitlement, engineering, legal or investor services; open questions listed here.
