# Competitive benchmark: what the Harrison Development site is missing

Date: 2026-11-09. Method: public homepages read through Firecrawl (about 38 credits). **Limits:** only homepages were read, only four sites gave useful data (East West Partners, Replay Destinations, Brookfield Residential, Slifer Smith & Frampton Development Sales). Urban Villages' domain is for sale (wrong site) and Douglas Elliman's page returned 404. Larger Colorado developers (Vail Resorts Development, Related Colorado, others) were not checked. This is a sample, not a market survey.

## What the closest competitor does (sliferdevelopmentsales.com)
- Headline "Colorado's Experts In New Development"; one call to action, "Work with us".
- Four phases: Concept + Strategy, Branding + Marketing, Listing + Sales, Closing + Beyond.
- Proof up front: **130+ projects brought to market, $6B+ sold**, eight named projects (Beaver Creek, Steamboat, Breckenridge, Keystone and others), and four developer testimonials.
- Claims to be "the only agency in the state" with a division solely for new development sales and marketing (their own claim; unverified).
- Names Vail Valley, Eagle County and Summit County. No team bios, no case studies, no market reports, no mention of AI.

## Developers' own sites (what clients are used to)
- East West Partners: homeowner testimonials, an Outside Magazine "Best Places to Work" award, news and stories, a team page. No lead form.
- Replay Destinations: named-project portfolio, news, media kits. No lead form.
- Brookfield Residential: "50+ communities, 40+ cities, 1,288 reviews" (third-party verified), a map, a blog, a legal footer on offers.

## Gaps, most important first
1. **Proof of track record.** Competitors lead with numbers, named projects and developer quotes. We have none, and must not invent any. Honest options: Charles's own verified sales history (from MLS records), a labeled private sample, or a founding-client pilot.
2. **Named markets.** We say "Colorado and the Rocky Mountain region." Competitors name counties and towns, which helps trust and local search. Needs a confirmed list of markets he will serve and is licensed in.
3. **A face and a story.** No portrait or fuller bio yet. Competitors are corporate, so a real founder is a differentiator.
4. **Lead capture beyond a form.** No booking link for a consultation call, and no downloadable resource. Competitors only have "contact".
5. **Content and insight.** No market reports, no case studies. Nobody sampled publishes them, so this is open ground. The weekly early-warning scan could feed public pipeline insights, but only from public sources.
6. **A "how we'd launch your project" sample.** A clearly labeled fictional or owner-approved microsite or deck. Doubles as the private pitch asset.
7. **Third-party validation.** No reviews, awards or press. Cannot be fabricated.
8. **Technical launch items.** Structured data (ProfessionalService schema), sitemap, canonical URLs, Google Business Profile. Held until launch by design.
9. **Short video.** No competitor has one; a 60-second founder video would stand out.

## Where we are already ahead
- Only site in the sample with a real inquiry form (with errors, honeypot and preview safety).
- No one mentions AI. "Teaches the AI" is open positioning.
- Clear services in plain language, a visible brokerage attribution and disclaimers, and a stronger mobile experience than the competitor sites sampled.

## Cautions
- Do not copy "only agency" style claims; they are exposure, not proof.
- Do not borrow competitors' numbers, project names or quotes.
