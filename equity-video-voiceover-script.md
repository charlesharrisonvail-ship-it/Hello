**Delivery notes**
- Flat and certain, not salesy. You're stating arithmetic, not pitching.
- Land hard on "stops" and "keeps working" — that contrast is the whole video.
- Leave a half-beat of silence before the last line.

**If you'd rather not record:** post it silent. LinkedIn autoplays muted and
the piece is built to read with sound off — captions aren't needed because the
words *are* the visuals. Silent is a legitimate choice here; a generic stock
music bed would be the worse option.

**What I could not do:** add a music bed from here. Higgsfield has no
standalone music model available to this session (its music engine is
restricted to the game pipeline), and generated audio lands on a CDN this
container is blocked from reaching, so I can't mux a track into the render
even if one existed. Adding audio is a 60-second job in CapCut on your phone
if you want a bed under your VO.
